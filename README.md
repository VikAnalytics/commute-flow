# CommuteFlow

CommuteFlow is a mobility-intelligence layer for where people live and where they stay.

Most property and travel tools optimize for price, rating, and distance.  
CommuteFlow optimizes for a harder and more valuable question:

**"How well can I move through the city from this location?"**

## Market Thesis

Cities are becoming more transit-dependent while housing and travel costs keep rising.
Users are increasingly willing to trade square footage or nightly rate for better time outcomes.

CommuteFlow turns transit complexity into a decision advantage:

- lower daily commute burden for residents
- higher attraction access for travelers
- clearer trade-offs between cost and mobility

## Why Now

- Public transit APIs and mapping infrastructure are mature enough for real-time route intelligence.
- Consumers are now used to decision support products that are explainable, not just ranked lists.
- Remote/hybrid work and urban tourism both increase demand for location flexibility with mobility certainty.

## Beachhead and Wedge

- **Beachhead 1 (Residential):** apartment search around workplace commute quality.
- **Beachhead 2 (Travel):** stay selection by multi-hub transit accessibility.

This creates a wedge into a broader "mobility-adjusted location intelligence" platform.

## Product Today

- **Apartments**
  - Enter workplace address.
  - Discover nearby apartments ranked by transit-informed commute outcomes.
  - See estimated travel time instantly.
  - Tap to reveal full journey details (line/vehicle/stops/direction when available).
- **Travel**
  - Enter city (Atlanta + Europe supported).
  - Auto-discover major hubs and score stays by connectivity.
  - Tap to reveal route details per hub in a compact card-first flow.

## Why It Wins

- **Transit-first ranking** vs. distance-first listing UX.
- **Explainable routing** (what line, what stop, what direction) vs. black-box score.
- **Mobile-native progressive disclosure**: compact cards, deep detail on tap.
- **Production-oriented stack** with backend proxy path and key-safety defaults.

## Expansion Path

- Add employer- and district-level commute benchmarks.
- Add portfolio intelligence for developers, brokers, and asset managers.
- Add city-level demand heatmaps based on mobility-adjusted desirability.
